GETTING HERE

Finding the wedding venue.

Lotte World is a large complex, so please allow extra time. The wedding venue is inside the Lotte World Folk Museum area on the 3rd floor.

DESTINATION

Lotte World Folk Museum · Traditional Wedding

240 Olympic-ro, Songpa-gu, Seoul, South Korea

Lotte World Folk Museum, 3rd Floor

FROM JAMSIL STATION

Follow these steps after Exit 4.

These steps follow Lotte World's official walking directions to the traditional wedding venue.

1
🚇

Jamsil Station → Exit 4

From Jamsil Station, follow signs for Exit 4. From the Exit 4 area, go straight toward the underground Lotte Mart Zettaplex.

↓
2
➡️

Follow Folk Museum / Lotte Mart signs

Continue straight, following signs toward the Folk Museum and Lotte Mart.

↓
3
↰

Turn left near CJ Gourmet Bridge

When you reach the area near CJ Gourmet Bridge, turn left toward the main gate.

↓
4
🎡

Pass Lotty & Lorry

You will see the Lotte World characters Lotty and Lorry. Pass them and continue around toward the back.

↓
5
🛗

Elevator → 3rd Floor

Take the Folk Museum elevator to the 3rd floor. This brings you to the Folk Museum / traditional restaurant area where the traditional wedding venue is located.

Important: Because the route inside Lotte World can be confusing, we recommend arriving 20–30 minutes earlier than you normally would.
