KOREAN WEDDING CUSTOM

Wedding Gifts in Korea

If this is your first Korean wedding, the gift tradition may feel a little different from what you are used to. At many Korean weddings, guests give a cash gift called chugui-geum (축의금) rather than bringing a physical registry gift.

01

Prepare an Envelope

Guests commonly place cash in an envelope and write their name on the outside so the family knows who the gift is from.

02

Check In Before the Ceremony

Before entering the ceremony, guests usually visit the wedding reception desk and hand over the envelope. The family or reception staff will record the guest's name.

03

Meal Ticket

At many Korean weddings, guests receive a meal ticket when they check in. The ticket is then used for the wedding buffet.

We are sharing this simply as a guide for guests who may be unfamiliar with Korean wedding customs. The exact process can vary slightly by family and venue.
